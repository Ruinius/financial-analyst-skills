## Qualitative Assessment

### Economic Moat

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Rating | Wide |
| Confidence | High |
| Source | Morningstar Equity Analyst Report, Dan Romanoff, CPA |
| Assessment Date | 2026-01-13 |

**Rationale:**
1. **Switching costs in Creative Cloud**: Adobe's Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects) is the de facto industry standard for creative professionals. Nearly all creative professionals rely on it, making it an insurmountable barrier to replace at an industry-wide level — individual users could switch, but would find themselves unable to collaborate with an industry that has standardized around Adobe.
2. **First-mover dominance in Document Cloud**: Adobe created the PDF file format and has maintained leadership with Acrobat. The ubiquity of PDF across every operating system and the lack of a truly competitive alternative to the format creates durable lock-in. E-signatures (Adobe Sign) further entrench the platform.
3. **Cross-selling flywheel via Digital Experience**: The digital experience segment (~25% of revenue) creates a natural cross-selling channel from content creation to marketing/advertising operations. With 22,000+ enterprise customers buying 5+ products, Adobe benefits from increasing engagement across its portfolio.

### EBITA Margin Outlook

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Direction | Expand |
| Magnitude | +2 pp over 5 years (~0.4 pp/year) |
| Confidence | Medium |
| Source | Morningstar Equity Analyst Report |
| Assessment Date | 2026-01-13 |

**Rationale:**
1. **Morningstar models ~200 bps of non-GAAP operating margin expansion over the next 5 years.** The primary driver is declining R&D intensity as generative AI solutions (Firefly) mature past the heavy investment phase.
2. **Already stellar margins limit upside.** Non-GAAP operating margin was 47.5% in Q1 2025. Management has demonstrated ability to maintain margins even during heavy AI investment cycles, suggesting disciplined cost control.
3. **AI monetization creates operating leverage.** The $125M AI ARR book (expected to double by year-end) represents incremental high-margin revenue that layers on top of the existing cost structure, supporting gradual margin expansion.

### Organic Growth Outlook

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Direction | Stable to slight decrease |
| Magnitude | -1 pp (from ~11% to ~10%) |
| Confidence | Medium |
| Source | Morningstar Equity Analyst Report |
| Assessment Date | 2026-01-13 |

**Rationale:**
1. **ARR guidance signals deceleration.** Management's fiscal 2026 ARR outlook calls for deceleration to 10.2% growth, down from recent ~11% constant currency growth. Morningstar's analyst flagged this as "the one obvious blemish" that is difficult to reconcile with otherwise positive data points.
2. **AI tailwinds are real but still early.** The $125M AI ARR is meaningful but constitutes <1% of total revenue. While Firefly, Acrobat AI Assistant, and GenStudio are gaining traction, they haven't yet moved the needle enough to offset natural deceleration in the mature Creative Cloud base.
3. **Pricing power provides a floor.** Adobe is implementing a tiered pricing structure tied to AI features for core applications, which should help sustain revenue growth even if volume growth moderates. Three consecutive quarters of revenue upside support a "stable demand environment" conclusion.
