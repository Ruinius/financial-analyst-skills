### Calculation Notes

- ROIC is negative because Invested Capital is negative (-1,170M). This is typical for capital-light subscription businesses like Adobe where deferred revenue (a current liability) dominates. Negative ROIC does NOT indicate poor performance — it means the business generates returns with negative net operating assets.
- EBITA (2,246) = Operating Income (2,163) + Amortization of acquired intangibles add-backs (+83 from GAAP reconciliation). SBC (469) stays in EBITA as it's classified as an operating cost.
- Amortization uses 0% marginal tax rate (non-deductible), so the adjusted tax rate is only slightly higher than effective (17.56% vs 17.00%) due to the $19M net non-operating income being tax-effected at 25%.
