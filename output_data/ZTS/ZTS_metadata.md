## Qualitative Assessment

_Last updated: 2026-05-07 | Source: 2025 Annual Report (10-K)_

### Economic Moat

| Field      | Value                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------------- |
| Rating     | **Wide**                        |
| Confidence | High                            |
| Source     | 2025 Annual Report (2026-02-13) |

1. **Intangible Assets (Patents & Innovation)**: Zoetis maintains a "first-in-class" lead with franchises like Librela and Solensia; new 2025 approvals Lenivia and Portela have patents extending into the 2030s.
2. **Switching Costs (Sales Force & Relationships)**: Global direct sales force of ~4,200 colleagues provides high-touch technical expertise, creating deep veterinarian reliance and high switching costs.
3. **Scale and Diversification**: Massive scale in R&D and manufacturing across 300+ product lines and 8 species provides cost advantages and stability that smaller competitors cannot replicate.

### EBITA Margin Outlook

| Field      | Value                |
| ---------- | -------------------- |
| Direction  | **Expand**           |
| Magnitude  | +2.0 pp over 5 years |
| Confidence | High                 |

1. **Product Mix Shift**: Favorable shift toward high-margin companion animal products (70% of total revenue) and improved manufacturing efficiency (COGS as % of revenue down to 28.2%).
2. **Pricing Power**: Demonstrated ability to raise prices by 4% in 2025, more than offsetting volume declines and FX headwinds.
3. **Operating Leverage**: ERP implementation ($29M in 2025) is a non-recurring headwind; as this subsides, significant operating efficiencies are expected to be unlocked.

### Organic Growth Outlook

| Field      | Value                |
| ---------- | -------------------- |
| Direction  | **Increase**         |
| Magnitude  | +2.0 pp over 5 years |
| Confidence | Medium               |

1. **Product Pipeline**: 12 potential blockbusters ($100M+ each) currently in the pipeline targeting major unmet needs.
2. **New Therapeutic Areas**: Aggressive expansion into high-growth white-space areas such as chronic kidney disease (CKD), oncology, and cardiology.
3. **Operational Momentum**: Achieved 6% organic operational growth in 2025, supported by 185 geographic expansions and lifecycle enhancements in a single year.
